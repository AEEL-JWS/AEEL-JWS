# AEEL · Korea University

Advanced Electronic Device & Energy Conversion System Lab.

We explore emerging materials and device architectures for sensing, memory, and energy conversion. Our work spans optoelectronic memristors, photodetectors, stretchable electronics, semiconductor devices, and photovoltaics.
