We explore next-generation display technologies integrating organic semiconductors, quantum dots, and perovskites. Through advanced material design, interface engineering, and device optimization, we aim to develop high-performance light-emitting and photodetection devices, enabling flexible displays, intelligent optical sensing, and bio-integrated applications for next-generation interactive and multifunctional displays.
