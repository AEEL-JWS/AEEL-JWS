We develop low-noise near-infrared (NIR) and short-wave infrared (SWIR) photodetectors based on organic and hybrid semiconductors. By optimizing energy-level alignment, charge-selective contacts, and interfacial trap states, we aim to suppress dark current and noise for highly sensitive infrared detection and imaging.
